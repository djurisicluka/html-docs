How the score works

Raw squeeze powder (SI 22%, CTB 14%, DTC 12%, float/liquidity 10%, catalyst 14%, momentum/social 8%) is then adjusted for fundamentals/cash runway (+10%), dilution/solvency/listing risk (−15%) and data confidence (±10%). Community heat is intentionally low weight because promotion can create false positives.
